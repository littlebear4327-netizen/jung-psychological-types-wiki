# Extraverted Thinking ($Te$)

## Overview
**Extraverted Thinking** ($Te$) is a rational judging function oriented predominantly toward objective data, external facts, and universally valid intellectual formulas `(pp. 334–335)`. The intellectual activity is synthetic and progressive, drawing its conclusions not from subjective inner premises, but from transmitted facts, external conditions, and objective tradition `(pp. 331–333)`.

## Key Formulation & Working Principle
- **Governing Law**: Subordinates all conscious activity to an objective criterion or intellectual formula (*formule générale*) believed to possess universal validity `(pp. 335–336)`.
- **Criterion of Truth**: Objective factuality and practical applicability. Ideas are valid only to the extent that they correspond to verifiable external reality `(pp. 332–333)`.
- **Opposing Axis**: Strictly represses [[Introverted-Feeling]] ($Fi$). Subjective valuations, personal emotional bonds, and aesthetic nuances are deemed irrelevant or obstructive to the objective formula `(pp. 336–337)`.
- **Mathematical / Structural Model**:
  $$ Te: \text{Conscious}(Thinking_{Object}) \longleftrightarrow \text{Unconscious}(Feeling_{Subject}^{Archaic}) $$

## Unconscious Compensation & Shadow Dynamics
- **Archaic Shadow**: Because subjective feeling is repressed, it degenerates in the unconscious into an archaic, infantile, and hypersensitive state `(pp. 336–338)`.
- **Eruption Manifestations**:
  - Resentful, personal touches in discussions; bitter personal polemics disguised as objective debate `(p. 337)`.
  - Suspicions of bad faith, envy, and moral pettiness directed against opponents `(p. 338)`.
  - Secret superstitions, bizarre mystical fixations, and "nothing but" (*rien que*) reductionism `(pp. 338–339)`.
- **Pathological Breakdown**: Fanatical tyranny, despotic enforcement of the formula, intellectual ossification, and compensatory neurotic breakdowns (hysterical bodily symptoms or obsessions) `(pp. 338–340)`.

## Known Trade-offs & Limitations
- **Strengths**: High organizing capacity, exceptional clarity in objective sciences, administrative efficiency, public leadership, and legal systematization `(pp. 334–336)`.
- **Deficits**: Total lack of empathy for subjective uniqueness, intellectual dogmatism, inability to recognize the personal equation, and suppression of spontaneous emotional life `(pp. 337–339)`.
