# Introverted Feeling ($Fi$)

## Overview
**Introverted Feeling** ($Fi$) is a rational judging function oriented toward subjective value, profound internal emotional resonance, and archetypal ideals `(pp. 371–374)`. Unlike extraverted feeling which diffuses outward, introverted feeling continually deepens and intensifies inward: "still waters run deep" `(p. 371)`. It seeks neither to influence nor to conform to others, but to maintain the inner integrity and sovereign purity of the self `(pp. 372–374)`.

## Key Formulation & Working Principle
- **Governing Law**: Subordinates feeling to the inner [[Subjective-Factor]]—an archetypal, often non-verbal standard of value and spiritual truth `(pp. 372–373)`.
- **Manifestation Style**: Outwardly calm, unpretentious, reserved, or even cold and inaccessible; inwardly passionate, intensive, and deeply moved by poetic or archetypal images `(pp. 371–374)`.
- **Opposing Axis**: Strictly represses [[Extraverted-Thinking]] ($Te$). Objective logical schemes, factual categorizations, and mechanical efficiency are felt as vulgar, invasive, and hostile to soul-values `(pp. 374–376)`.
- **Mathematical / Structural Model**:
  $$ Fi: \text{Conscious}(Feeling_{Subject}) \longleftrightarrow \text{Unconscious}(Thinking_{Object}^{Archaic}) $$

## Unconscious Compensation & Shadow Dynamics
- **Archaic Shadow**: Because extraverted thinking is repressed, thinking becomes primitive, petty, tyrannical, and conspiratorial `(pp. 374–376)`.
- **Eruption Manifestations**:
  - Secret despotic bossiness and subtle emotional manipulation behind a facade of gentle passivity `(p. 375)`.
  - Obsessive rumination over what others are thinking, feeling secretly persecuted or plotted against `(pp. 374–375)`.
  - Petty, captious, and spiteful intellectual criticism directed against rivals or authority figures `(p. 375)`.
- **Pathological Breakdown**: **Neurasthenia** (*neurasthenia*) or anaemia, accompanied by profound apathy, melancholic withdrawal, and secret despotic outbursts `(p. 375)`.

## Known Trade-offs & Limitations
- **Strengths**: Incorruptible moral integrity, profound empathy without sentimentality, poetic resonance, and silent ethical witness `(pp. 372–374)`.
- **Deficits**: Inaccessibility to others, tendency toward aloof coldness, complete inability to justify feelings objectively, and susceptibility to secret tyrannical pride `(pp. 373–376)`.
