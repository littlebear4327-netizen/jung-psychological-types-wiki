# Introverted Thinking ($Ti$)

## Overview
**Introverted Thinking** ($Ti$) is a rational judging function oriented toward subjective ideas, internal logical coherence, and primordial intellectual structures rather than objective facts `(pp. 365–367)`. While extraverted thinking builds outward toward the object, introverted thinking digs inward toward its subjective foundation, seeking not broad factual accumulation, but intensive depth, clarity of concept, and foundational truth `(pp. 366–368)`.

## Key Formulation & Working Principle
- **Governing Law**: Subordinates cognition to the inner [[Subjective-Factor]]—the a priori archetypal idea or structural necessity of thought `(pp. 365–367)`.
- **Criterion of Truth**: Internal consistency, structural purity, and profound conceptual clarity. Facts are secondary; if facts do not align with the subjective idea, the attitude is: *"Tant pis pour les faits"* (so much the worse for the facts) `(p. 367)`.
- **Opposing Axis**: Strictly represses [[Extraverted-Feeling]] ($Fe$). Conventional social values, emotional pleasantries, and external consensus are regarded with indifference, disdain, or dread `(pp. 368–370)`.
- **Mathematical / Structural Model**:
  $$ Ti: \text{Conscious}(Thinking_{Subject}) \longleftrightarrow \text{Unconscious}(Feeling_{Object}^{Archaic}) $$

## Unconscious Compensation & Shadow Dynamics
- **Archaic Shadow**: Because extraverted feeling is repressed, feeling remains archaic, hypersensitive, and undifferentiated in relation to outer objects `(pp. 369–371)`.
- **Eruption Manifestations**:
  - Touchiness, morbid sensitivity to criticism, and paranoid suspicion of rivals or colleagues `(pp. 369–370)`.
  - Social awkwardness, misanthropy, and acute vulnerability to crude emotional manipulation by unsuitable persons `(p. 370)`.
  - Bitter polemical defensiveness: presenting ideas with aggressive obstinacy and aloof contempt to shield raw personal feelings `(pp. 369–371)`.
- **Pathological Breakdown**: **Psychasthenia** (*psychasthénie*) or schizophrenia; severe isolation, obsessive rumination, and paralyzing anxiety over object relations `(pp. 364, 368–371)`.

## Known Trade-offs & Limitations
- **Strengths**: Unsurpassed profundity, conceptual rigor, original philosophical formulation, and structural independence from intellectual fads `(pp. 366–368)`.
- **Deficits**: Inability to communicate ideas accessibly, disregard for empirical verification, interpersonal awkwardness, and vulnerability to embittered misanthropy `(pp. 368–371)`.
