# Extraverted Feeling ($Fe$)

## Overview
**Extraverted Feeling** ($Fe$) is a rational judging function oriented predominantly toward external objective values, shared cultural standards, and collective interpersonal harmony `(pp. 341–343)`. Feeling in this type is not an arbitrary subjective mood, but a differentiated, objective act of valuation that aligns the subject with the social ethos, tradition, and collective feeling-values of their milieu `(pp. 342–344)`.

## Key Formulation & Working Principle
- **Governing Law**: Subordinates feeling to the demands of objective situations and shared cultural norms (e.g., loving what is respectable, beautiful, or socially sanctioned) `(pp. 342–343)`.
- **Criterion of Value**: Concordance with collective harmony and interpersonal rapport. The goal is to establish pleasant, resonant, and harmonious relationships with the objective world `(p. 343)`.
- **Opposing Axis**: Strictly represses [[Introverted-Thinking]] ($Ti$). Logical critique, skeptical analysis, and detached cold intellectual reasoning are felt as disturbing, tactless, and destructive to feeling-harmony `(pp. 344–346)`.
- **Mathematical / Structural Model**:
  $$ Fe: \text{Conscious}(Feeling_{Object}) \longleftrightarrow \text{Unconscious}(Thinking_{Subject}^{Archaic}) $$

## Unconscious Compensation & Shadow Dynamics
- **Archaic Shadow**: Because subjective thinking is repressed, it remains archaic, obsessive, and infantile in the unconscious `(pp. 344–346)`.
- **Eruption Manifestations**:
  - Cold, ruthless, and cynical "nothing but" (*rien que*) thoughts undermining the authenticity of relationships `(p. 345)`.
  - Secret obsessiveness: analyzing trivial matters, harbouring captious criticisms, or suspecting base motives in loved ones `(pp. 345–346)`.
  - Theatricality and hollow sentimentality: when feeling is pushed to excess, it loses genuine personal warmth and becomes an artificial, theatrical pose `(p. 344)`.
- **Pathological Breakdown**: **Hysteria** is the neurosis par excellence; unconscious counter-thoughts break into consciousness as hysterical attacks, dissociative states, and somatic conversion `(pp. 345–346)`.

## Known Trade-offs & Limitations
- **Strengths**: Exceptional social tact, capacity to create community cohesion, warmth, diplomatic grace, and cultural transmission `(pp. 342–344)`.
- **Deficits**: Vulnerability to conventional superficiality, terror of nonconformity, incapacity for rigorous independent critique, and suppression of authentic individual identity `(pp. 344–346)`.
